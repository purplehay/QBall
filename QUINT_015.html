Ray marcher

SuperRT / cone experiments

Binary shadows use a single lit/blocked result. Cone-aware marching increases the tolerated feature size with distance according to the pixel footprint. RGB555 + Bayer dithering is a display treatment, not an optimisation.

Ray traced reflections

One reflected SDF ray per reflective pixel. Compare FPS with the toggle off; reduce ray steps or render scale if needed. Matte objects remain matte.

Camera

Isometric orbit uses parallel projection. Drag empty space to orbit and use the mouse wheel or pinch to zoom. Drag a shape to move it. Free flight: WASD move; Q/E down/up; Shift sprint. Arrow keys also turn. On a phone use the movement pad and drag the world. H hides the editor.
